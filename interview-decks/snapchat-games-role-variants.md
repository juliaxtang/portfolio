# Snapchat Games — role variants

Games is always the **second** case, so it doesn't get its own framing slide — the framing slide at the start of the call belongs to Topic Chats (the anchor). Games just needs:

1. A **handoff line** at the start (transition from Topic Chats)
2. A few **tie-back speaker notes** on key slides
3. A **role-specific close**

---

## Patreon — community / organic discovery

### Handoff line (start of slide 1)
"The second case is shorter and sits in a different domain — but the through-line is the same. I want to show you how I treated games not as entertainment, but as a community feature. As a reason for friends to talk."

### Tie-back speaker notes

- **Slide 5** (Two jobs): dwell on "Connections and Conversations." Add: *"This is the Patreon-relevant job. People didn't want a games destination — they wanted a reason to message a friend. Games were that reason."*
- **Slide 6** (three initial bets): add: *"Putting games inside the conversation, not next to it, is what moved the needle. Community features compound when you put them where the conversation already is."*
- **Appendix A3** (Daily Challenges misread): if it comes up, this is your strongest Patreon-relevant lesson. *"I scoped this as a solo habit loop. The data said something different — the lift was in group sends, not solo returns. Even a solo activity becomes community fuel when it gives people a reason to message. That's the thread I'd bring forward at Patreon."*

### Close (after slide 12)
"Closing line: Games scaled because we treated them as a community feature, not as entertainment. That's the lesson I'd bring to Patreon — community products don't grow by getting better at the activity, they grow by getting better at giving members a reason to engage with each other."
