01 · Foundation

Design principles

Four ideas that decide every visual choice. If a decision violates one, the decision is wrong.

Quiet, not silent

Restraint is the default. Color and motion arrive only when they earn their place.

Iridescent, not loud

Color shifts like light through glass. One brand gradient does the heavy lifting.

Editorial, not decorative

Typography carries personality. A serif italic does the singing; a technical sans does the talking.

Accessible by default

Every text/background pair clears WCAG AA. Most clear AAA. Brand color is decoration, never the message.

Three levels of emphasis. The brand gradient stays in atmosphere — CTAs use ink that flips to powder blue on hover.

Three levels of emphasis — no gradient buttons. The brand gradient lives in atmosphere (hero, featured surfaces); CTAs use ink. Primary buttons hover with a soft powder-blue shadow lift instead.
