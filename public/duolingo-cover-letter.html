Dear Duolingo Hiring Team,

I speak five languages and am working on a sixth. Duolingo is a big part of how that started, and not in a surface way. Language learning is genuinely daunting for most people: you can't measure your own progress, you feel incompetent for a long time, and the activation energy to even begin is enormous.

What Duolingo figured out, and what I think is underappreciated as a design achievement, is that it solves the cold-start problem. Short lessons, immediate feedback, XP that makes you feel capable before you technically are: it decouples perceived progress from actual fluency, and that's what gets people through the door. It worked on me. I've since graduated to platforms built for intermediate and advanced learners, but Duolingo did exactly what it needed to: it made the first step feel possible. That's the product I want to spend my career on.

At Snapchat I own design for Games as a Staff Product Designer — defining the vision, building the exec case, shipping to 30M+ DAU, and developing the designers around me. Getting there required holding both ends of the range at once: setting the long-term strategic frame while also being the person who sweats a transition curve or a line of microcopy until it's right.

That combination is what I find most satisfying about design work, and it's where I think I'd contribute most at Duolingo. I care as much about the unexpected moments of delight that make a product feel alive as I do about the business logic underneath them. AI is part of how I get there — I use Cursor and Claude daily across the full design process, and have shipped AI-driven features at Snap.

Before Snap I was at Tinder for three years, where I shipped Festival Mode zero-to-one in two months with engagement that more than doubled the main experience, and led Tappy Cloud, a backend-driven profile system that has since powered 100+ experiments and an estimated $50-110M in revenue lift. The through line across all of it is consumer products where the design has to earn the habit, not just enable the function.

Half a billion learners is a number that actually means something to me. I'd love to talk about what the team is building.

Warmly,
